# An edit renders only the tiles it changed

List: a Remove, Heal or Clone edit recomputes only the 64 px tiles it reaches on the GPU, and the loupe converts, fits and uploads only those, with a whole render's pixels; `perf/111-dirty-render`.

## What it does

- **Tiles.** Each view keeps, per output size, every stage of its last GPU render (the resampled source, the linear image, its planes) and now its finished 8-bit output. A spot edit takes the boxes `spots_keep` rewrote (spots taken off, drawn, put back) as the linear image's dirty tiles (64 × 64 px). Each later stage grows them by its reach and recomputes only its dirty tiles, from a cut of its kept input with that margin, into a copy of its kept output. The per-pixel stage runs on the union of the planes' dirty tiles.
- **Reach at a 2560 px loupe**: noise reduction 5–45 px (process V6's joint colour noise reduction, on by default for raws: about 170 px, its band halo on every side, with the whole frame's noise model), texture and sharpening 5–30 px, Highlights / Shadows about 250 px, Clarity about 200 px, Dehaze about 150 px across and 460 px down, local Moiré / Noise about 90 px. In the app, a Heal spot on a 33 MP A7 V raw at a 2560 × 1707 loupe recomputed 64–100 of 1080 tiles (6–9 %); the tests' scene with every plane on and the joint colour noise reduction, 224–324 (21–30 %, a move counts its old place and its new one; an undo that brings two spots back, 512).
- **Same bits as a whole render.** Every filter is local (box blurs, medians, resampling taps, pointwise kernels). The vertical box blur starts its runs on the whole image's rows (`box_v` takes the cut's first row) and sums each window directly for radii up to 7 (`BOX_DIRECT_MAX`: a run of 16 rows tripled the reach on the guided filters' small grids); a cut is resampled with the whole image's taps, sliced. Tests compare every incremental result with a whole render pixel for pixel.
- **Dropped work still fails.** A render that starts from the last output first clears its dirty rectangles (`rect_clear`), so a dispatch that never runs there leaves alpha 0, which the readback and display checks catch; the fault test `Fault::DropWork` may start from the last render for that reason.
- **What renders whole** (and is kept for the next edit): anything but the spots changed (the settings without the spots, the request, the source info, the plan's eyes, upright, recolor references, window and size: `dirty::Rest`); the spot redo did not start from the image the kept linear stage was made from; the airlight of Dehaze moved (only the per-pixel stage); the joint colour noise reduction measuring its noise on the image itself (a render of the whole frame that is one noise tile), which a spot changes (only the linear stage); a float render (`F32Hdr`); and in stage 1, masks that read the image (ranges, Sky and Subject heuristics, Auto Mask, Refine Edges), red eye, LaMa spots and the camera's local tone map.
- **The CPU renderer** keeps whole renders.
- **Memory**: the kept output is 4 bytes a pixel per size (17 MB at 2560 × 1707), counted in the stage cache's bytes, so the trim frees it with the rest (the local Moiré / Noise chromaticity plane, 12 bytes a pixel, is counted too).
- `LIGHTCRAFT_PROFILE=1` prints `gpu dirty: <why>` and `gpu tiles: <n> of <all>` per render.
- **Measured** (release build, one A7 V raw, 5 Heal spots, a move and a resize, app open throughout, other builds running): the GPU render's stages, each waited for (`LIGHTCRAFT_PROFILE`), went from a median of 105 ms (60–222) to 56 ms (28–93). Release-to-result did not change beyond the noise (clicks: median 160 ms before, 171 ms after), because most of each loupe job is now CPU work after the GPU render (about 80–120 ms in those conditions), not measured stage by stage here.
- **The loupe job after the render (stage 2).** The GPU renderer says which output a render started from and which rectangles changed (`lightcraft_gpu::take_frame_note`). The loupe job keeps, with the view's stages and under that render's number (`lightcraft_gpu::keep_host`), what it made of it on the host: the image converted for the display, its sRGB twin (view cache and histogram) and the image fitted to the drawn size. The next job converts and fits only the changed rectangles over copies of those (`engine::media::dirty`): the conversions map each pixel on its own and the Lanczos fit gives each output pixel from fixed taps in a fixed order, so the bits are the whole job's. The result says which rectangles of its picture changed (`RenderResult::changed`), and the window uploads only those into the loupe texture (`set_partial`) when the texture shows the picture they changed from. A job copies its picture to keep it only when the next job can start from it (the render is numbered and the view has stages), and keeps only the images the next job starts from: the converted image when it converted for a display, the sRGB twin, and the fit when the host made it (a device fit or nothing converted keeps only the sizes it is checked against). They count in `GpuStages::bytes`, so the stage trim frees them. When the changed rectangles cover more than half of the picture, the job fits it whole (`fit_for_display`, on every core; same bits). A GPU render that fails in any way (a panic, a recorded failure, the device failing on another thread) lets the view's stages go and leaves no note, so no CPU fallback is patched with it. The timing lines read a clock only under `LIGHTCRAFT_PROFILE`, through `web_time` (`engine::media::dirty::clock`): `std::time::Instant` panics on the web.
- **Measured, stage 2** (same raw, 10 Heal clicks, the three builds run back to back, medians): release → result 116.5 ms on `custom`, 92 ms after stage 1, 84.5 ms after stage 2; the Lanczos fit 15.7 → 3.2 ms; the worker job 74.7 → 52.1 → 42.4 ms. Step by step before stage 2: [comment on #111](https://github.com/techneissa/lightcraft/issues/111#issuecomment-6727983986). What is left: the GPU call (~30 ms, of which spots and noise reduction ~11 ms, mostly whole-buffer copies) and two frame waits (~16 ms from the click to the job, up to 16 ms from the job to the screen).
- `LIGHTCRAFT_PROFILE=1` also prints the job's steps (`job:`), the worker's (`worker:`), and the UI's (`ui: spot.add`, `submitted`, `texture set … whole / N rectangles`), with times since start to follow one edit through the threads.
- **Not yet**: reading back only the dirty tiles; the histogram patched instead of counted again; the frame waits; dirty tiles through masks that read the image, red eye and the LaMa layer; the CPU renderer; drag drafts.

## Hooks in official files

- `crates/gpu/src/render.rs`: `linear_stage` returns a `Linear` (with custom's `colour_noise`); `joint_halo`, `joint_band` and `nrcj` are `pub(crate)` for the dirty joint colour noise reduction; `GpuStages::bytes` counts `Planes.chroma`; `Entry` keeps the spots image its linear image came from (`post`) and the last render (`last`); `render` starts from it (linear stage, planes, the per-pixel stage over the dirty rectangles with `X0`) and keeps it on success; `linear` returns the image before noise reduction and what the spots redrew; `box_pass` takes the cut's first row and `box_chunk`; `gaussian_at`, `guided_at`, `guided_coeffs_at`, `resize_with`, `pack_taps` (the whole-image functions call them); `Planes` and `Prep` fields `pub(crate)`.
- `crates/gpu/src/spots_keep.rs`: `spots` also returns the boxes it rewrote.
- `crates/gpu/src/wgsl/blur.wgsl` (`box_v`: P[5], the first row), `wgsl/finish.wgsl` and `params.rs` (`X0`), `ctx.rs` (the `rect_copy` module).
- `crates/gpu/src/lib.rs`: `mod dirty`, its exports, the frame note cleared after a failed render.
- `crates/gpu/tests/it/main.rs`: `mod dirty_render`. `crates/gpu/src/render.rs` also: `GpuStages::host` (the job's kept images), cleared with the rest.
- `crates/engine/src/media/render.rs`: `RenderResult::changed`; the loupe job converts and fits through `dirty::Patch` and keeps what it made; timing lines. `media.rs`: `pub mod dirty`. `display.rs`: `histogram_sample` is `pub(crate)`.
- `changed: None` in every other `RenderResult` literal: `crates/engine/src/media/{render,quick}.rs`, `crates/engine/src/slide_title.rs`, `crates/ui-egui/src/render.rs` (tests), `apps/lightcraft-web/src/workers.rs`.
- `crates/ui-egui/src/render.rs`: `Renderer::shown_frames`, `upload` (whole or the changed rectangles), `sub_image`, `stamp`, timing lines. `panels/detail/retouch.rs`: a timing line around `spot.add`.
- `crates/gpu/src/lib.rs`: `Fault::BrokenElsewhere`; every failed render clears the view's stages and the note. `crates/ui-egui/src/render.rs`: `release_closed_loupe` takes a `web_time::Instant`.
- `crates/gpu/tests/it/fallback.rs` (`dropped_work_in_an_incremental_render_falls_back`, `a_render_failed_by_another_thread_leaves_no_note`) and `mask_cache.rs` (`the_kept_chroma_plane_counts_with_the_stages`): a test each.

## Tests

`fork/tests.d/dirty-render.txt`. They count the tiles each edit's per-pixel stage computes (`lightcraft_gpu::tiles_rendered`) after add, move, resize, hide, delete, undo, the same settings again and a global slider, in the fit view and in a zoomed window, and compare every result with a whole render pixel for pixel; one compares with the CPU within the GPU / CPU bounds. None measures time.
