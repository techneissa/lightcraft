# macOS Colour

## What it does

macOS Colour (Mac only): the system's raw decoder (Core Image `CIRAWFilter`) makes a raw's colours and LightCraft keeps tone, white balance and every slider. A third Profile choice (a row, the notes under it, greyed where this computer can't make it), the default for new raws (Settings ▸ Import ▸ Starting Colour for Raw Photos), Apply macOS Colour to All Photos from This Camera…, AI Denoise through a per-photo colour transfer, Kelvin white balance from the decoder's as-shot white, no camera-JPEG flash, presets that carry it. A photo set to `macos` that the Mac can't decode renders as Camera Matching and the Edit panel says why. ARW only so far (the survey of other makers is in `docs/camera-preview-colour.md`).
