# Relight

List: Relight, a Develop section with a light you drag on the photo that brightens what is near it in depth and faces it, from the photo's own depth map or an on-device depth model, live while you drag (issue #288), the ⌥-drag preview that shows where the light reaches (issue #294), up to eight lights, each with its own handle, size ring and colour (issue #299), a type for each light (issue #300), and a gel, a pattern and haze for each light (issue #302). Branches `feat/288-relight`, `feat/294-relight-alt-overlay`, `feat/299-relight-several-lights`, `feat/300-relight-light-types`, `feat/302-relight-gels-patterns-haze`.

## What it does

- **Relight** section in Develop (between Lens Blur and Calibration): the list of lights (Light 1 … Light 8, each with its colour and Amount, a click selects one; **Add Light**, **Duplicate**, **Delete**), a small depth picture of the photo with every light on it (the selected one ringed), a Near–Far track for the selected light's depth, its **Amount**, **Softness**, **Warmth**, and an **Advanced** flyout with **Darken Surroundings** (the whole photo's) and the selected light's **Wrap**. While the section is open and the lights change the photo (some Amount or Darken Surroundings above 0, the section's eye on), every light's handle sits on the photo; one drag is one undo step.
- **Several lights (issue #299):**
  - **Up to 8 lights**, at least one (a new photo has one at Amount 0, as before). Each has its own place, depth, Amount, Softness, Warmth and Wrap; Darken Surroundings is one value for the section and darkens what the combined light doesn't reach. Delete on the only light puts it back to its defaults.
  - **Selection** is UI state (`Session::relight_selected`, one photo at a time, never saved in the edit, not an undo step; an index past the last light reads as the last). The sliders, the depth track and the shorthand controls act on the selected light.
  - **On the photo:** a press on a handle selects that light (a click on an unselected one only selects it) and a drag from it moves it; the selected handle has a ring (radius 24 points at Softness 0 to 64 at 100) with a grip: dragging the ring sets its Softness (one undo step, "Relight Size"); a scroll over a handle moves that light nearer (up) or farther by 5 of 100 per wheel notch, the rest of a slow trackpad scroll kept between frames (the view doesn't take that scroll; a run of scrolling is one undo step, closed after 0.4 s of quiet, and a scroll during a drag of a handle changes nothing; where handles overlap a press takes the one drawn on top, the selected one and then the later one; on the web build the handles and the scroll claim are inert: `detail/relight.rs`). In the depth box a press near a handle selects it.
  - **Adding up:** each light's `min(falloff · facing / peak, 1)` over the field is its **lobe** (`Field::lobe`; it does not depend on Amount, Warmth or Darken Surroundings), made once per light and kept (12 kept, most recently used first and a hit moves to the front, so every step of a drag finds the other lights' lobes; 6.8 MB each at 1600 × 1067: `pipeline::relight::LOBES`), so dragging one light makes one lobe, not eight. `pipeline::relight::forget()` frees every kept lobe and shading; the engine calls it when its field store lets a photo's field go, and the idle-memory work can call it too. The lobes add in scene-linear light, `ambient + Σ amountᵢ · lobeᵢ`, and the roll-off to the ceiling and the shadow hold work on that sum (so eight lights on one spot don't pass white); the combined shading (`Field::shading_of`) is kept too (4). Along depth edges the sum is refit against the photo like a single light's.
  - **Colour:** each light has a tilt (`warmth_tilt`). A pixel's colour is the mix of the lights' tilts, each weighted by the light it gave the pixel (`Σ addedᵢ · tiltᵢ / Σ addedᵢ`), times the new lights' share of its light. The finish stage's local temperature takes one plane per mask, so the colour goes up as two: one for the warm tilts (temperature the strongest warm tilt, each light weighted by its tilt over it) and one for the cool ones, which add where a warm and a cool light overlap. A photo with lights of one side only makes one plane, as before.
  - **⌥ view:** shows the combined light (one shading for all the lights), tinted by the lights' Warmth averaged by Amount; the selected light's handle keeps its ring above the dimming and its name ("Light 2") is drawn beside it.
  - **Old edits:** a block saved before this (one light's numbers and `darken` in one object) reads as one light and renders the same, byte for byte (checked by hand against the build before this change on ten renders: white, warm with Darken Surroundings, cool and tight, Darken Surroundings alone and warm and broad, each whole and cropped).
- **Light types (issue #300):** each light has a type, picked from the menu on its row in the list (a name and a chevron left of its Amount; a pick selects the light and sets `relight.<n>.kind`). A new light and every light saved before types are a **Point**, rendered byte for byte as before (the test `a_point_light_is_the_formula_it_always_was` compares its lobe with the old formula to the bit). The types' shading is in `crates/relight/src/kinds.rs` and only makes each pixel's `falloff · facing`; everything after it (the lobe, Amount, Warmth, the roll-off, the colour planes, the ⌥ view, CPU and GPU) is the point light's, so every type adds up with the others and renders alike everywhere. Positions are normalized photo coordinates, as the point light's.
  - **Spot:** the point light through a cone: its place (the handle), an **aim** (`spotAimX`, `spotAimY`: a second dot on the photo; the cone points at the surface under it, by the field's depth there), the cone's half-angle **Cone Angle** (`spotAngle`, 5–85°) and **Cone Edge** (`spotEdge`, 0 hard – 100 the whole cone fades). Drag the grip on the cone's edge to change the angle (on the photo the cone's lines are drawn at the angle itself, not foreshortened by depth). It keeps Softness (the source's size and fall-off), Warmth, Wrap and the Near–Far depth.
  - **Sun / Window:** one direction, no fall-off with distance, facing only: `sunAzimuth` (−180..180°: 0 from the camera's side, 90 from the right, 180 from behind the scene) and `sunElevation` (0° horizon .. 90° overhead). The handle is the arrow's base; the arrow points at the sun and its length is the height (the longest arrow is a sun overhead, a short one a low sun in front of the scene; at least 26 points drawn); drag its tip to set both (a drag keeps the sun in front of the scene: a hollow tip is a sun behind it, set by the slider). No depth: the section has no Near–Far control for a Sun / Window (nor for a Rim or a Fill). Softness makes the terminator gentler. A light that becomes a Sun / Window with Wrap still at its default starts at **Wrap 0** (surfaces turned away get nothing; `relight.<n>.kind` and `relight.addLight` do it, a saved file is read as saved); raise Wrap and they keep some. The Amount is what a surface that fully faces it gets (a fixed peak: no photo-wide normalization).
  - **Softbox:** a rectangle on the photo at the light's depth, facing into the scene (a surface nearer than it gets nothing from it): its middle is the handle, `boxWidth` and `boxHeight` (shares of the photo's width and height, 0.02–1.5) and `boxRotation` (degrees, clockwise). A corner grip resizes it and a knob above the top edge turns it. Each pixel sums the light of a 4 × 4 grid of points across it (each as a small round source, facing and cosine of the emitter), so a bigger box is softer and wraps more round shapes. No Softness.
  - **Rim:** no place (no handle); lights the near side of depth edges (a step of at least 12 % of depth within the reach, nearer counting for more) where the surface turns away from the camera: `rimWidth` (0–100) sets the reach (0.8–4.3 % of the long side) and how far it turns. A step counts fully at the nearest ring and less farther out, and a surface facing the camera still gets half of it, so the edge reads along its whole length: at full Amount and the default width the lit edge gains about 1.5–2 stops (p6, ⌥ pictures). Fixed peak. No depth, no Wrap.
  - **Fill:** no place; lifts the shadows by the luminance the photo shows (the guide's plus the photo's Exposure, as the grade reads it; the lobe is kept per Exposure): full in the deepest shadows (−6 stops under grey), fading linearly to nothing at Softness's top (0.5 to 2.5 stops: a little over mid-grey at the default 50), at a third of the point light's strength so that full Amount lifts the deepest shadows of a face-on wall by about 1.4 stops and mid-grey by about 0.5. More on near things than far: its weight falls linearly with depth from the nearest to the farthest (`fillNear` 0 alike .. 100 the farthest gets nothing); from the camera's side (Wrap softens the facing). Fixed peak.
  - **Saved:** per light, `kind` (the type's name: `spot`, `sun`, `softbox`, `rim`, `fill`; left out for a Point) and each type's number that differs from its default: `spotAimX`, `spotAimY`, `spotAngle`, `spotEdge`, `sunAzimuth`, `sunElevation`, `boxWidth`, `boxHeight`, `boxRotation`, `rimWidth`, `fillNear`. The numbers of a type the light isn't stay with it, so switching back finds them. Read leniently (`KindSettings::read`): a kind this build doesn't know, or one that isn't a name or a number, reads as a Point; an index past the last reads as the last type; a number that isn't one is its default; every number stays in its range. Fork catalog format 14: a fork 13 build drops these fields and would open such a library as Points, so the format moved on.
  - **Controls:** `relight.<n>.kind` (0 point, 1 spot, 2 sun / window, 3 softbox, 4 rim, 5 fill; a whole number), `relight.<n>.spotAimX`, `.spotAimY`, `.spotAngle`, `.spotEdge`, `.sunAzimuth`, `.sunElevation`, `.boxWidth`, `.boxHeight`, `.boxRotation`, `.rimWidth`, `.fillNear`; the shorthands (`relight.kind` …) act on the selected light. The section shows only the selected light's own: Point Amount, Softness, Warmth; Spot those and Cone Angle and Cone Edge; Sun those and Sun Direction and Sun Height; Softbox Amount, Warmth, Box Width, Box Height, Box Angle; Rim Amount, Warmth, Rim Width; Fill Amount, Softness, Warmth, Near Bias. `relight.addLight` takes `kind?` (a name or 0..5); `relight.status` lists each light with its `kind` (omitted for a Point).
  - **On the photo:** a Rim and a Fill have no handle on the photo or in the depth box (select them in the list; ⌥'s picture names the light in the photo's corner). The size ring (Softness) is the point light's only. A drag of a type's handle is one undo step and ⌥ while it is held shows the combined light as for any control (`relight_alt`; `dragging_control` is the handle's control id). Own files: `panels/edit/relight/kinds.rs` (the menu, the controls by type), `panels/detail/relight/kinds.rs` (the handles).
- **Effects per light (issue #302):** an **Effects** flyout row after Advanced in the section, for the selected light. All drawn in code, no file read (`crates/relight/src/effects.rs`).
  - **Gel:** a colour for the light, any hue and a strength: five named gels as swatches (Warm Amber 38°/70, Teal 178°/65, Magenta 305°/65, Deep Blue 232°/75, Red 0°/75; `relight.setGel {gel}`, which also takes `hue` and `strength`, strength 0 takes it off) and **Gel Hue** (0–360°, 0 red, 120 green, 240 blue) and **Gel Strength** (0–100). Warmth stays: the light's colour is the gel's multiplied with Warmth's. The gel is `hue_colour ^ (0.8 · strength^1.6)` per channel (no change at 0, a strong colour at 1; a channel never goes under 0.12 of the light, so a gel stays finite). **How it reaches the render:** `Effects::light_colour` turns that colour into the finish stage's own local temperature and tint (`(1 + 0.3 t, 1 − 0.22 s, 1 − 0.3 t)`, luminance kept), the pair Warmth already used, so a gel goes up the same mask planes and the CPU and the GPU read one finish kernel and cannot differ. The colour planes grew from two (warm, cool) to four (warm, cool, magenta, green; `Shading::split`, ids `relight::IDS`), each made only when some light has that component, so a photo without a gel makes the same two planes as before. Lights mix as before: a pixel's tilt and tint are the lights' weighted by the light each gave it.
  - **Pattern:** the light shines through blinds, window panes, leaves or dapple (`relight.<n>.pattern` 1–4; 0 none), with **Scale**, **Rotation** (−180–180°) and **Softness**. The pattern is a projector's slide at the light: a surface point takes the pattern's value at the direction it lies from the light (`Field::pattern_plane`; tangent coordinates about an axis into the picture, out of it for a light behind most of the scene), so stripes run straight over a wall facing the camera, converge over a floor, jump at a step in depth and wrap a leg. A surface seen at a grazing angle to the light widens the edges (the depth model's normals); beside or behind the light's plane the pattern fades to its own mean coverage (half for blinds, 0.74 for panes, the baked texture's mean for leaves and dapple). Leaves and dapple are dear per pixel (scattered shapes), so each is baked once into a tileable texture (16 cells across, 24 texels a cell, one at its own softness and one with edges three times as wide for grazing surfaces; cached by pattern and softness, the newest four) and sampled bilinearly during the projection; blinds and panes stay analytic. Measured once by hand on a 1600 × 1067 field, release, one pattern plane: blinds 8 ms, panes 8–13, leaves 54 → 11, dapple 31 → 10 (the light's own lobe is another 7 ms). The pattern multiplies the light's lobe, so the facing term, the roll-off and the colour work as before; the patterned lobe is kept like the plain one (its key adds the pattern's numbers, none for a light without one). The shading's key hashes each light's effects with the light's place in the list, so the same effect on another light is another shading.
  - **Haze:** a soft glow around the light that grows with the distance in depth: `Field::haze_lobe` is a halo around where the light sits on the photo (radius from Softness) times a fog that is 0 at the nearest depth and 1 at the farthest. It is one more lobe of the same light and colour (`Part` with `HAZE_GAIN` = 0.25 of the light's strength at Haze 100), so it adds in the same sum, rolls off the same way and takes the gel. It brightens like the light does (an exposure on what is there), so it does not lift pure black.
  - **Old edits:** a light without effects saves exactly as before (the new keys are left out at their default) and renders to the byte as before (hashed twelve renders on #307's head and on this: white, warm with Darken Surroundings, cool and tight, Darken Surroundings alone, warm and broad, two lights, each whole and small).
  - **⌥ view:** shows the combined light, so the pattern, the haze and the gel's reach are in it (crosses cluster in the lit gaps; contours wrap them).
- **Depth** is found off the UI thread by one worker (the newest request first, at most 12 waiting), shown as "Finding depth" in the top-bar activity ring, cancellable there: the photo's own depth map when it has one (an iPhone Portrait HEIC), else the depth model. It is kept in memory while it is among the most recently used (300 MB: a field holds up to 36 bytes a pixel, 52–92 MB at 1600 pixels, so 3–6 photos), so moving the light or editing the photo never finds it again; a photo pushed out of that set, or any photo after a restart, finds it again (nothing is saved to disk). Until it is ready the section shows Finding Depth…; without the model it offers Download Depth Model (asks once, then shows progress). A finding cancelled in the ring is not started again by renders; the section shows Find Depth (`relight.findDepth`).
- **⌥ while dragging** any Relight control (Amount, Softness, Warmth, Darken Surroundings, Wrap, the Near–Far track, the light's handle on the photo or in the depth box) shows where the combined light reaches, over the loupe at any zoom and crop, with the photo dimmed a little under it (never replaced, and no render change: `Overlay::for_control` has nothing for `relight.*`). Two looks, picked by the **Crosses / Contours** switch under the Near–Far track (`UiState::relight_look`, `relightLook`, synced; a value this build doesn't know reads as Crosses and keeps the other settings, a settings file with one is refused):
  - **Crosses:** a grid of + marks (about 30 points apart on screen, snapped to a ladder of grid sizes so a pinch doesn't rebuild it at each step) lying on their surfaces: the two arms are the surface's own axes from the field's normal, foreshortened as the photo shows them; their size and brightness come from the light's gain in stops as a share of the light's peak (`overlay::peak_stops`, from `PEAK_GAIN`: three stops; absolute, so they follow Amount), tinted amber, deeper for a warm light. Where Darken Surroundings darkens there are hollow rings, also on their surfaces.
  - **Contours:** lines of equal gain at +0.1 +0.2 +0.35 +0.5 +0.7 +0.9 +1.2 +1.5 +1.8 +2.2 +2.6 +2.9 stops and, where something darkens, −0.25 −0.5 −0.75 −1 −1.5 and a dashed line at 0 where darkening starts; marching squares on the shading at most 320 pixels on a side, blurred and rounded, each level labelled once on its longest line.
  - **Speed:** `lightcraft_relight::overlay` builds the geometry (normalized photo coordinates) from the shading the render makes (`pipeline::relight::shading_for`: once per set of lights, shared with the render's memo) on one worker thread that takes the newest request only; the UI draws the newest ready geometry as egui shapes, the one for the light on screen when it is ready, else the last of the same photo (a frame or two behind), never one of another photo. Measured once by hand with `LIGHTCRAFT_PROFILE=1` on a 1600 × 1067 field, release: crosses 0.1–0.2 ms, contours 2.3 ms (4.7 at most) on the worker, plus the shading when the render hasn't made it (about 19 ms, the same one the render then reuses); the UI thread makes the shapes in 0.02–0.07 ms (crosses) and 0.07–1.4 ms (contours).
- **Web:** the web build has no Relight (the depth would be found on the page's thread): the section says Relight needs the desktop app.
- **The depth model** is Depth Anything 3 Mono Large (code and weights Apache-2.0, about 1.34 GB), run in pure Rust with candle (Metal on macOS). It downloads into the shared models folder (`da3-mono-large/`) and is never in the repository; `LIGHTCRAFT_DEPTH_MIRRORS` adds mirrors. Without the `sam` feature the model is not built in and only files' own depth maps work.
- **The field** (`crates/relight/src/field.rs`): the depth is upsampled to a 1600-pixel guide of the photo by a joint bilateral filter that takes a weighted median across depth steps, so edges take one side (no halo, no stair step). The depth model draws a floor or wall as flat terraces with small steps between them, so before anything else the depth is smoothed over a window of 6 % of the long side by a guided filter against the photo's brightness, and a pixel takes that where it is within 4–11 % of its own depth; steps above about 11 % of depth (the cat against the floor) stay sharp, and smaller ones on one colour soften, where the light is smooth anyway (issue #293). Normals come from that depth, guided-filtered; slopes steeper than 88° (depth steps, not surfaces; a floor seen from the ground leans well past 80°) are left out and the rest is smoothed.
- **The light** (`crates/relight/src/light.rs`; each of up to eight lights): a point at the handle's place on the photo and the track's depth. Each pixel gets light by its 3D distance to the point (Softness widens the falloff: a tight light also falls off faster) and a facing term (Wrap softens it: at 0 a surface turned away gets nothing), normalized to the brightest pixel. Full Amount is three stops at the most lit point (a mid-grey one gains about 2.25: the roll-off holds from 1 stop over grey up and is off below 0.3 stops under it; between, the gain is cut to what fits under the ceiling, so nothing goes past it); full Darken Surroundings takes the rest down by more than three stops. It works in scene-linear light, as an exposure before the tone curve. Highlights roll off to a ceiling just under white and deep shadows are held back, so noise isn't lifted. Warmth is a colour temperature from about 16000 K to 2700 K, weighted by the light's share of each pixel (the share is `1 − (1 − lit)²` of the light's part of the pixel, so the colour reaches a little further than the brightness). Along real depth edges (what is left of the steps after the terraces are smoothed) the shading is refit against the photo's own luminance.
- **Preview equals export:** the light's place is in normalized photo coordinates (before lens corrections and crop, as a mask's) plus a depth, and the shading is made once per set of lights at field resolution and read by each render at its own pixels, on CPU and GPU.
- **Speed** (measured once by hand with `LIGHTCRAFT_PROFILE=1`, not a standing check): a drag makes one shading per position (8 ms at 1600 px, measured once after #293 with a 1067 × 1600 photo; 8–13 ms before it) and each render's planes (3.4 ms at 2.5 MP).
- **Settings:** `fork_relight` in develop settings, `{"lights": [{amount, softness, warmth, x, y, depth, wrap, gelHue?, gelStrength?, pattern?, patternScale?, patternRotation?, patternSoftness?, haze?, kind?, spotAimX?, spotAimY?, spotAngle?, spotEdge?, sunAzimuth?, sunElevation?, boxWidth?, boxHeight?, boxRotation?, rimWidth?, fillNear?}, …], "darken": n}` (the effects' keys only where one differs from its default: 0, 0, 0, 50, 0, 30, 0), written only when some value differs from its default (a light moved but at Amount 0 is written), fork catalog format 14 (13 wrote the lights without the types, which an older build drops; 12 wrote the lights without the effects, which an older build drops; 11 wrote one light's numbers and `darken` in one object; that reads as one light). A log written under a fork 11 (or older) snapshot names that first form in its patches: replay puts the photo's block in the first form before applying such a patch (`Replay::first_form_relight`), so the edit lands on the first light; a History written then reads the same way. A preset or partial block of the first form (flat numbers: an older preset, `develop.merge`) lands on the first light and Darken Surroundings (`DevelopSettings::merged`), the other lights staying; a block in the new shape replaces the photo's lights (it has a `lights` list, which replaces the list).
  - **No log of two formats:** if the upgrade's snapshot can't be written when a library opens (a nearly full disk), the journal appends nothing under the older snapshot (`Journal::upgrade_pending`; `append` is an error, so the edit stays in memory and the UI shows its failed-save notice). The engine retries the snapshot before the next append (after a 2 s back-off, not mid-interaction); it is a snapshot of memory, so it saves the queued edits, and the log carries on in the new format. Reopening before that gives the older files as they were. (Tagging each record with its form would leave an older build misreading a log it can't tell is mixed.) Read leniently: a block that is not an object, or `lights` that is not a list, reads as the default; a light that is not an object (or has a wrong-typed number) reads as a default light in its place; at most 8 lights are kept and at least one; every number is kept in its control's range. A later part adds fields to a light (a kind, a gel, a pattern) with a default each, so this shape reads on. The sliders are controls: `relight.<n>.amount`, `.softness`, `.warmth`, `.x`, `.y`, `.depth`, `.wrap` for light `n` (from 0) and `relight.darken`; `relight.amount` … (without `n`) are the same controls for the **selected light** in the commands (`develop.set`, `develop.adjust`, `develop.controls`; the develop crate itself reads them as light 0). Undo and the control channel reach them. Saved Relight edits render stronger with the same numbers since #293 (full Amount is three stops, was two).
- **Where the light travels:** it is one photo's own mark, like masks and spots. Keep in Sync leaves it out; Portrait presets leave it out; Copy Settings offers the Relight group but does not tick it by default; a develop preset carries it only when its Relight group is ticked.
- **Commands** (and so MCP): `relight.addLight {amount?, x?, y?}` (Amount 50 in a free spot, selected), `relight.duplicateLight {index?}` (a little aside, after the light, selected), `relight.deleteLight {index?}` (selects the one before; deleting the first selects the one that follows), each one undo step and an error at 8 lights or for a light that isn't there, and `relight.selectLight {index}` (`index` is required; no undo step); a `develop.set` or `develop.adjust` of `relight.<n>.…` for a light the photo doesn't have is an error and makes no undo step; `develop.quickAdjust` of the shorthands acts on each photo's selected light; `relight.status {id?}` (the lights, Darken Surroundings, the selected index, depth ready, being found, cancelled, its source, the model's state), `relight.findDepth {id?}` (an error while the light is off) and `relight.downloadModel {confirm: true}`.
- Tuning outside the app: `cargo run --release -p lightcraft-depth --example depth -- <model dir> photo.jpg depth.png --dump dump/`, then `cargo run --release -p lightcraft-relight --example relight -- photo.jpg dump/ out [x y depth amount softness warmth darken wrap]`.

## Known limits

- A spot's hard edge (Cone Edge 0) leaks a little onto steep, evenly toned walls: the edge-band refit (a guided filter against the photo's luminance along depth steps) mixes the two sides' light there. By design; a soft edge hides it.
- A Rim follows the depth model's edges: on a staircase it also lights step edges; it is cleanest with a subject against a plain background.
- Haze sits at the light's place, so a Rim or a Fill (no place) takes none.

- A pattern swaps sides when a light's depth crosses the scene's mean depth: the aim into or out of the picture is picked by comparing the light's depth with the scene's mean (`Field::pattern_plane`), so the pattern jumps there (redo in #316).

## What's New entry

### Relight
- A new Develop section, Relight, adds a light you place on the photo. Drag its handle and the light reaches what is near it in depth and faces it, live while you drag. Amount, Softness and Warmth shape it; Darken Surroundings and Wrap are under Advanced.
- Relight holds up to eight lights: Add Light, Duplicate and Delete under the list, click a row or a handle to pick one, drag the ring around the picked handle to change its size and scroll over a handle to move it nearer or farther. The lights add up, each with its own colour, so a warm key and a cool fill work together.
- Each light has an Effects group: a gel (five named ones, or any hue and strength) that colours its light, a pattern (blinds, window panes, leaves or dapple) it shines through, laid over the surfaces so it bends over steps and faces, and haze, a soft glow that grows with distance.
- Hold ⌥ while dragging a Relight control to see where the light reaches: small crosses lying on the surfaces, or lines of equal light (a switch under the Near–Far track picks one).
- Depth comes from the photo's own depth map (iPhone Portrait photos) or from an on-device depth model, downloaded once (about 1.3 GB). Saved with the edit; like masks and spots it stays on its photo unless you tick Relight in Copy Settings or a preset. The export matches the preview. The web version has no Relight.
