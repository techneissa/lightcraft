# Relight

List: Relight, a Develop section with a light you drag on the photo that brightens what is near it in depth and faces it, from the photo's own depth map or an on-device depth model, live while you drag (issue #288), and the ⌥-drag preview that shows where the light reaches (issue #294). Branches `feat/288-relight`, `feat/294-relight-alt-overlay`.

## What it does

- **Relight** section in Develop (between Lens Blur and Calibration): a small depth picture of the photo with the light on it, a Near–Far track for the light's depth, **Amount**, **Softness**, **Warmth**, and an **Advanced** flyout with **Darken Surroundings** and **Wrap**. While the section is open and the light changes the photo (Amount or Darken Surroundings above 0, the section's eye on), the light's handle sits on the photo and can be dragged there; one drag is one undo step.
- **Depth** is found off the UI thread by one worker (the newest request first, at most 12 waiting), shown as "Finding depth" in the top-bar activity ring, cancellable there: the photo's own depth map when it has one (an iPhone Portrait HEIC), else the depth model. It is kept in memory while it is among the most recently used (300 MB: a field holds up to 36 bytes a pixel, 52–92 MB at 1600 pixels, so 3–6 photos), so moving the light or editing the photo never finds it again; a photo pushed out of that set, or any photo after a restart, finds it again (nothing is saved to disk). Until it is ready the section shows Finding Depth…; without the model it offers Download Depth Model (asks once, then shows progress). A finding cancelled in the ring is not started again by renders; the section shows Find Depth (`relight.findDepth`).
- **⌥ while dragging** any Relight control (Amount, Softness, Warmth, Darken Surroundings, Wrap, the Near–Far track, the light's handle on the photo or in the depth box) shows where the light reaches, over the loupe at any zoom and crop, with the photo dimmed a little under it (never replaced, and no render change: `Overlay::for_control` has nothing for `relight.*`). Two looks, picked by the **Crosses / Contours** switch under the Near–Far track (`UiState::relight_look`, `relightLook`, synced):
  - **Crosses:** a grid of + marks (about 30 points apart on screen, snapped to a ladder of grid sizes so a pinch doesn't rebuild it at each step) lying on their surfaces: the two arms are the surface's own axes from the field's normal, foreshortened as the photo shows them; their size and brightness come from the light's gain in stops (absolute: they follow Amount), tinted amber, deeper for a warm light. Where Darken Surroundings darkens there are hollow rings, also on their surfaces.
  - **Contours:** lines of equal gain at +0.1 +0.2 +0.35 +0.5 +0.7 +0.9 +1.2 +1.5 +1.8 stops and, where something darkens, −0.25 −0.5 −0.75 −1 −1.5 and a dashed line at 0 where darkening starts; marching squares on the shading at most 320 pixels on a side, blurred and rounded, each level labelled once on its longest line.
  - **Speed:** `lightcraft_relight::overlay` builds the geometry (normalized photo coordinates) from the shading the render makes (`pipeline::relight::shading_for`: once per light, shared with the render's memo) on one worker thread that takes the newest request only; the UI draws the newest ready geometry as egui shapes, the one for the light on screen when it is ready, else the last of the same photo (a frame or two behind), never one of another photo. Measured once by hand with `LIGHTCRAFT_PROFILE=1` on a 1600 × 1067 field, release: crosses 0.1–0.2 ms, contours 2.3 ms (4.7 at most) on the worker, plus the shading when the render hasn't made it (about 19 ms, the same one the render then reuses); the UI thread makes the shapes in 0.02–0.07 ms (crosses) and 0.07–1.4 ms (contours).
- **Web:** the web build has no Relight (the depth would be found on the page's thread): the section says Relight needs the desktop app.
- **The depth model** is Depth Anything 3 Mono Large (code and weights Apache-2.0, about 1.34 GB), run in pure Rust with candle (Metal on macOS). It downloads into the shared models folder (`da3-mono-large/`) and is never in the repository; `LIGHTCRAFT_DEPTH_MIRRORS` adds mirrors. Without the `sam` feature the model is not built in and only files' own depth maps work.
- **The field** (`crates/relight/src/field.rs`): the depth is upsampled to a 1600-pixel guide of the photo by a joint bilateral filter that takes a weighted median across depth steps, so edges take one side (no halo, no stair step). Normals come from a guided-filtered depth; slopes steeper than 80° (depth steps, not surfaces) are left out and the rest is smoothed.
- **The light** (`crates/relight/src/light.rs`): a point at the handle's place on the photo and the track's depth. Each pixel gets light by its 3D distance to the point (Softness widens the falloff) and a gentle facing term (Wrap softens it), normalized to the brightest pixel. It works in scene-linear light, as an exposure before the tone curve. Highlights roll off to a ceiling under white and deep shadows are held back, so noise isn't lifted. Warmth is a colour temperature from about 12000 K to 2800 K, weighted by the light's share of each pixel. Along depth edges the shading is refit against the photo's own luminance.
- **Preview equals export:** the light's place is in normalized photo coordinates (before lens corrections and crop, as a mask's) plus a depth, and the shading is made once per light at field resolution and read by each render at its own pixels, on CPU and GPU.
- **Speed** (measured once by hand with `LIGHTCRAFT_PROFILE=1`, not a standing check): a drag makes one shading per position (8–13 ms) and each render's planes (3.4 ms at 2.5 MP).
- **Settings:** `fork_relight` in develop settings, written only when some value differs from its default (a light moved but at Amount 0 is written), fork catalog format 11. The sliders are controls `relight.amount`, `.softness`, `.warmth`, `.x`, `.y`, `.depth`, `.darken`, `.wrap`: undo and the control channel reach them.
- **Where the light travels:** it is one photo's own mark, like masks and spots. Keep in Sync leaves it out; Portrait presets leave it out; Copy Settings offers the Relight group but does not tick it by default; a develop preset carries it only when its Relight group is ticked.
- **Commands** (and so MCP): `relight.status {id?}` (depth ready, being found, cancelled, its source, the model's state), `relight.findDepth {id?}` (an error while the light is off) and `relight.downloadModel {confirm: true}`.
- Tuning outside the app: `cargo run --release -p lightcraft-depth --example depth -- <model dir> photo.jpg depth.png --dump dump/`, then `cargo run --release -p lightcraft-relight --example relight -- photo.jpg dump/ out [x y depth amount softness warmth darken wrap]`.

## Tests

`fork/tests.d/relight.txt`:

- `lightcraft-relight`: malformed or missing depth is an error, not a panic; a depth model's map rounded to its 14-pixel patches fits 16:10, 1.85:1 and 2.35:1 photos, while a file's own map must fit within 2 % (a 200 × 160 map on a 4:3 photo is refused, and so is a portrait's map on a landscape photo); a flat wall faces the camera and a receding floor tilts up; a step between near and far is no surface; disparity reads as depth the other way round; the filters (box mean, guided filter, joint bilateral upsampling, bilinear); near things catch more light than far ones; facing; the Amount goes to the most lit point and white is not passed; off is nothing and Darken darkens what the light misses; the light's colour fades smoothly where Darken meets the light; Warmth is a temperature; hostile numbers give finite planes.
- `lightcraft-depth`: input sizes keep the aspect in whole patches; resizing; the sky takes the far end; broken output and no model are errors; f16 and f32 tensors; hostile headers; the model is one pinned file.
- `lightcraft-develop`: the block is left out while default and read back when set; a malformed block reads as off and numbers stay in range.
- `lightcraft-pipeline` (`tests_relight`): no light, no field or the section's eye off changes no byte, and the near square brightens more than four times the wall; a draft and a 4× export scaled down make the same light, also cropped, and the light stays on the same place under a crop.
- `lightcraft-gpu` (`--test it -- relight`): the GPU equals the CPU within 3 levels of 8 bits (measured 1 on this Mac): white, warm and darkened, cool and cropped, after a mask.
- `lightcraft-engine` (`--features sam`): a failure is kept and returned to a waiting export; fields are kept most recently used first within the budget; a cancelled finding is not a failure, renders don't restart it and Find Depth makes it; an export batch keeps the field it was set up with while its other photos push it out of memory; the loupe's view key changes when the field lands; the light stays on its photo (Keep in Sync, default Copy Settings, Portrait presets); a panic while an export's renders are set up leaves no flag set; Find Depth does nothing while the light is off; a missing or broken depth file.
- `lightcraft-relight` (`overlay`): contour lines run at their level, closed where they fit in the plane and ending on its edge where they don't; no lines and no NaN from an empty, one-pixel, mis-sized or NaN plane.
- `lightcraft-ui-egui` (`edit_sections`): the section order. (`settings_sync`: `relightLook` is decided.)

## What's New entry

### Relight
- A new Develop section, Relight, adds a light you place on the photo. Drag its handle and the light reaches what is near it in depth and faces it, live while you drag. Amount, Softness and Warmth shape it; Darken Surroundings and Wrap are under Advanced.
- Hold ⌥ while dragging a Relight control to see where the light reaches: small crosses lying on the surfaces, or lines of equal light (a switch under the Near–Far track picks one).
- Depth comes from the photo's own depth map (iPhone Portrait photos) or from an on-device depth model, downloaded once (about 1.3 GB). Saved with the edit; like masks and spots it stays on its photo unless you tick Relight in Copy Settings or a preset. The export matches the preview. The web version has no Relight.
